How it works

Four weeks from sprint kick-off to agents at work.

  1. Assessment

    We map processes, tools and data to find repetitive, rule-based work that takes the most hours.

    ~2 weeks

  2. Sprint

    We build the agents, connect them to your systems and run them in “shadow mode” next to your team until the results are right.

    4 weeks

  3. Run & grow

    We monitor quality, measure hours saved and add automations. Everything stays yours — no vendor lock-in.

    ongoing
