Our approach

Brokers arrive late. We start early.

Traditional brokers are paid on completed transactions, so they engage when an owner is ready to list. We flip the model: owners pay a monthly retainer for preparation and a success fee only if a sale completes with our involvement.

01 — Structural gap

The pre-exit window is underserved.

Value is created 1.5–3 years before a sale — by cleaning up numbers, reducing owner dependency and fixing risks. That is exactly where most advisors are not economically motivated to work.

02 — Boring businesses

Stable cash flow beats hype.

HVAC and maintenance services, car repair shops, laundries, hospitality, light manufacturing. Predictable earnings, low cyclicality and real value at exit.

03 — AI as a team

Senior attention, lean costs.

AI agents handle research, data processing and first drafts under human review. Founders focus on relationships, judgement and negotiation.

Exit Readiness Program

Six modules, each with its own output and impact on valuation.

  1. Exit Readiness Assessment

    Readiness score, map of weaknesses ranked by impact on price, and an 18-month roadmap.

    4 weeks

  2. Quality of Earnings

    Normalised EBITDA, revenue quality and working capital — a financial picture that survives due diligence.

    6 weeks

  3. SOP Package

    Documented processes and handover of key relationships, so the business runs without the owner.

    3 months

  4. Risk Mitigation

    Legal, financial and operational risks identified and addressed with lawyers and tax advisors before buyers look.

    ongoing

  5. Confidential Information Memorandum

    Teaser and CIM at the standard expected by funds and strategic buyers.

    8 weeks

  6. Valuation Tracking

    Quarterly revaluation showing how preparation adds value — and when to go to market.

    quarterly
